Key Analytical Insights
  • Seed Quality is the #1 Yield Driver: Seed_Quality_Score showed the strongest positive correlation with Yield_Tonnes_Ha across all crops and seasons. Investment in certified high-quality seed is the single highest-ROI intervention.
  • Drip Irrigation Maximizes Both Yield and Profit: Drip-irrigated farms consistently achieved the highest average yield and water efficiency (t/1000m³), despite representing a higher initial capital cost.
  • Rainfall Optimal Range for Top-Quartile Yields: Farms in the top 25% yield tier operated within a specific rainfall band (Q1–Q3 range of high-yield cohort). Farms outside this band showed notably reduced performance.
  • Flood Irrigation Creates Systemic Water Waste: Flood-irrigated farms consumed significantly more water_m3 per tonne of yield produced — the worst water efficiency ratio of all four irrigation categories.
  • Disease & Pest Risk Negatively Impacts Yield: Farms with Disease_Pest_Risk_pct ≥ 60% recorded materially lower mean yields compared to low-risk farms (<30%) — validating the importance of proactive pest management protocols.
  • Fertilizer Use is Positively Stratified by Yield: Top-quartile yield farms applied significantly more fertilizer per hectare than bottom-quartile farms, confirming that nutrient management directly differentiates performance tiers.
  • ~20% of Farms Operated at a Net Loss: A material proportion of farms reported negative Profit_INR, concentrated in the Rainfed irrigation cohort and Millet crop category — indicating structural vulnerability in these segments.

Recommendations & Future Work
Recommendation 01

Prioritize Drip Irrigation Adoption

Policy and farm investment should promote transition from flood irrigation to drip systems. Data confirms drip delivers the highest yield-per-water-unit ratio and profitability, directly addressing water scarcity concerns.

Recommendation 02

Seed Quality Certification Programme

The strongest predictor of high yield is seed quality score. A state-level certified seed distribution programme targeting low-yield farm clusters would provide the highest marginal return on agricultural investment.

Recommendation 03

Targeted Disease Risk Intervention

Farms with ≥60% disease/pest risk recorded sharply lower yields. Predictive risk scoring models using environmental inputs (humidity, temperature, rainfall) could enable pre-emptive intervention before yield loss occurs.

Recommendation 04

Machine Learning Yield Prediction

The rich 28-column dataset is well-suited for supervised regression modelling (Random Forest, XGBoost) to predict farm-level yield before harvest — enabling real-time resource reallocation and financial planning for farmers.

Recommendation 05

Loss-Farm Intervention Strategy

~20% farms operated at a net loss. Segmentation analysis by crop type and irrigation method reveals that rainfed + millet combinations are the highest-loss cohort — a clear target for subsidised support programmes.

Recommendation 06

Geo-Spatial State-Level Analysis

The dataset includes State and District columns which were used for top-10 state ranking. A geo-spatial choropleth mapping layer would visually identify regional yield clusters and underperforming zones for policy targeting.
